Architecture

One guest seat per client. Full isolation.
Each client is a guest on their own page under the HQ workspace. Each page holds that client's own full Content Pipeline database (same schema for everyone) plus their own dashboard. No client can ever see another. A new client is provisioned from one template: page + database + dashboard.

The one human decision · section 5

Open a card → approve, comment, or reject
This is the whole thesis. The client opens the In Review card on their phone, sees the post and the on-brand image, and makes one decision. Reject sends it back to the Brain with their comment as the instruction, so the next draft is better.
why competitors all sound the same is not a skincare problem. It is a routine problem.
If you are Overwhelmed Olivia, you have heard this a hundred ways. Here is what actually moves the needle for your skin, without the 10-step routine. Pick three things, do them every day, give it four weeks. Simple beats fancy. That is the Lumen Skin Studio way.

The dashboard · section 6

Per-client KPIs, chosen from a standard menu
Everyone has the same database. The dashboard is the part that changes per client. At setup you pick which KPIs matter to that client and the dashboard is built from them. Live data wiring (GA4 / GSC / social / Stripe) is a documented seam for later; the structure is built now.
