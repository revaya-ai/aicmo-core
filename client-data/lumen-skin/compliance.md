# Lumen Skin — Compliance Rules

Cosmetic products may not make drug claims. The following phrases imply treatment, cure, or clinical
substantiation that would classify the product as a drug under FDA guidelines. Flag any copy that
contains them so a human can rewrite before publishing.

## banned claims
cure
cures
heal
heals
clinically proven
fda approved
treats
prevents disease
reverses aging
eliminates wrinkles
eliminates acne
kills bacteria
kills germs
anti-inflammatory
reduces inflammation
dermatologist tested
hypoallergenic
repairs damaged skin
restores skin barrier
