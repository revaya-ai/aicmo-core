# Strategy

The Brick chain is the reasoning method the AI CMO uses to turn one seed idea into one on-brand post. Five decisions run in order. Each decision locks before the next begins. The output is four artifacts handed to the persistence layer.

## The chain: five steps in order

### Phase 0: Voice of Customer
Run before Intake. Load the voice of customer signal from the client context. Read brand-and-audience.md to identify the audience persona, the real pain points, and the actual phrases customers use. Lock the VoC signal. Every step below references it.

- Intake restates the seed in the customer's language.
- Angle targets a real pain point.
- Hook borrows a real phrase.

The VoC phase cannot be skipped. A post that does not start with the customer's actual words will fail to land.

### Step 1: Intake
Restate the seed idea in one plain line. Strip it to the single thing it is really about. Cut everything that is not the core. Lock that line.

### Step 2: Topic and pillar
Write a one-line topic. Name which content pillar it serves. The pillar must come from the client strategy file. If the idea fits no pillar, reshape it until it does. Lock the topic and pillar together.

### Step 3: Angle
Load the angle-library. Choose one named angle that creates real tension for this audience and pillar. Lock the angle name and a one-line reason it fits. Do not start on the hook until the angle is locked.

### Step 4: Hook
Load the hook-library. Write line one only. Five to ten words. Pick one named hook pattern and fill it with the real topic so it opens a gap the reader needs to close. No throat-clearing. No "in today's world." Lock the hook.

### Step 5: Story and shift
Load the story-structures and voice-os libraries. First pick one named body arc and outline it in three or four beats. Then write the body to that arc. Run the voice-os self-check. The body is not locked until the self-check passes.

## Locking rules

- Never write the body before the hook is locked.
- Never skip a step. Each step depends on the output of the one before it.
- If a later step exposes a weak earlier decision, go back, fix it, and re-lock forward from that point.

## Output contract

Hand exactly these four locked artifacts to the persistence layer:

- pillar
- angle
- hook
- body

Nothing else. The artifacts are persisted at status drafted.

## Why this order matters

The order is not arbitrary. Topic before angle prevents generic takes. Angle before hook ensures the first line is pointed at something real. Hook before body ensures the body does not drift from what the hook promised. VoC before all of it ensures the whole chain is grounded in actual customer language, not brand assumptions.
