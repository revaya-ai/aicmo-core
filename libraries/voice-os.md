# Voice OS

The master voice rulebook. Every post passes through these rules before it is locked. These rules apply to all clients unless the client voice file explicitly overrides a specific rule. The client voice always wins over defaults.

## Hard rules

- NO em dashes as sentence breaks. No en dashes as sentence breaks. No hyphens used as sentence breaks. Use commas or periods instead.
- No emojis. None in any post, caption, hook, or body.
- Always "I" for personal brand voice. Never "we" unless the brand is run by two or more named people and that is explicit in the client voice file.
- Every claim needs a number, a name, or a date. If you cannot back it, cut it. Never invent a statistic.
- One idea per post. If a second idea appears, save it for a separate post.
- Show, do not claim. "Our cleanser is effective" is a claim. "Our cleanser cleared hormonal acne in 14 days for 83% of testers" is a fact.
- Write for one reader. Speak directly to them using "you."
- Read the client voice file before writing anything. The brand voice always overrides these defaults.

## Banned phrases

Cut these on sight. They are the fingerprints of generic AI copy and will be trimmed on every review pass.

- in today's fast-paced world
- it's important to note
- at the end of the day
- when it comes to
- unlock (as in "unlock your potential")
- leverage
- elevate / elevate your routine
- game-changer
- revolutionary / revolutionize
- dive in / let's dive in / deep dive
- delve
- in conclusion / in summary
- the world of (as in "the world of skincare")
- look no further
- tried and true
- nestled
- testament to
- navigate the landscape
- supercharge
- harness the power of
- furthermore
- moreover
- additionally (as a filler opener)
- hence / thus
- in essence / fundamentally / at its core

## Self-check before locking any body

Answer each. If any answer is no, fix the body and re-check.

1. Zero em dashes, en dashes, or hyphens used as sentence breaks?
2. Zero banned phrases anywhere in the body?
3. Every claim backed by a specific number, name, or date, or already cut?
4. One idea only, not two?
5. First line earns the read, no throat-clearing opener?
6. Sentence lengths vary, no robotic rhythm of identical shapes?
7. Reads in the client's voice, not a generic brand voice?
